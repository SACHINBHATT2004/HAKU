HAKU'S TREASURE HUNT — 3D birthday game
==========================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the file first.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Safari.
   (Works offline. With internet, the nicer font loads too.)
3. Press "Start the hunt" and keep the sound on.

HOW TO PLAY
- Phone: touch the left side of the screen for the joystick,
  drag on the right side to turn the camera, tap "Jump" to jump.
- Laptop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, drag with the mouse to turn the camera,
  Space to jump, Q/E also turn the camera.
- Follow the signboards: village -> bridge -> flower fields -> hill ruins.
  The glowing light pillar marks the key. 20 optional stars along the way.
- When you pick up the key, the treasure chest drops from the sky and opens,
  your birthday video plays, then "Happy Birthday HAKU" rises out of the chest.

THE BIRTHDAY VIDEO AND THE CAT
- Your video and your cat picture are built right into index.html,
  so they always work, even if you open index.html on its own.
- The video plays by itself when the treasure opens.
  On iPhone, Safari may ask for one tap: press the "Play video" button.
- To use a different video or cat picture, send it to me and I will build it in.

CHANGE THE NAME OR MESSAGE
Open index.html in Notepad / VS Code. Near the top, in CONFIG:
    name: 'HAKU',
    message: '...',

OPTIONAL SONG
Put a file named birthday.mp3 in the "sounds" folder and it plays after the video.

SHARE ONLINE
Drag the whole folder onto Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) to get a link.
